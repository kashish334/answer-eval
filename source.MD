## Answer key / rubric for questions attempted

### Q21 (Section B, 2 marks)
**Describe any two features of arid soils.**

Key points (any 2 for full marks):
- Sandy in texture
- Saline in nature
- Low humus content / poor moisture retention
- Can be cultivated with proper irrigation

### Q22 (Section B, 2 marks)
**Explain the effectiveness of horizontal distribution of power in the context of India.**

Key points:
- Power is shared among different organs at the same level (legislature, executive, judiciary)
- Each organ checks and balances the others
- Prevents concentration of power in one hand / ensures accountability

### Q23 (Section B, 2 marks)
**What changes did the Government of India make in its economic policies in the beginning of 1991? Explain.**

Key points:
- Liberalisation — removal of barriers/restrictions on foreign trade and investment
- Privatisation of public sector
- Globalisation — integration with the world economy
- Reduction of import tariffs, encouragement of FDI / MNCs, Special Economic Zones

### Q24 (Section B, 2 marks)
**Mention any two economic impacts of the First World War on India.**

Key points:
- War expenditure financed by loans and increased taxes (customs duties, income tax introduced)
- Prices of goods increased sharply (near doubling 1913–1918), causing hardship
- Widespread famines and the influenza epidemic that killed millions

### Q25 (Section C, 3 marks)
**How does democracy establish an accountable, responsible and legitimate government? Explain.**

Key points:
- Accountable — government answerable to citizens through elections
- Responsible — transparency, rule-following, right to information
- Legitimate — government of the people, based on majority consent, even if slower
